For every $1 Medicare paid for open heart valve surgery, the typical hospital billed $5.47.

Minnesota hospitals billed $4.16. Figures are median charge-to-payment ratios across 792 hospital and procedure-category pairs at 420 U.S. hospitals.

The gap shows up in every type of valve stay

Median bills range from $171,348 to $444,968 depending on the procedure category. Medicare's median payment is a small fraction of that in each one.

Within categories, the median ratio runs from 4.8× for the least complex stays (DRG 221) to 5.8× for stays with complications (DRG 220).

Minnesota bills less than most of the country

Minnesota's 22 hospital and procedure pairs, from 12 hospitals, have a median ratio of 4.2×, compared with 5.5× nationally.

No Minnesota pair tops 6.2×. Across the rest of the country, 38% of pairs do.

State medians run from 2.8× in Massachusetts to 9.7× in Nevada

Not counting Maryland, Minnesota has the seventh-lowest median of the 42 states with enough data to compare. The dashed line marks the national median of 5.5×.

Maryland sets hospital rates for all payers, so its charges and payments are regulated to be close. Its 1.2× isn't comparable to other states.

Seven states and D.C. had fewer than 5 hospital and procedure pairs and are left out: AK, DC, DE, HI, RI, SD, VT, WY.

Catheter-based replacement shows a similar gap

TAVR replaces the aortic valve through a catheter instead of open surgery. Its median ratio is 4.9×, somewhat below open surgery's 5.5×.

About this data

Source
CMS Medicare Inpatient Hospitals by Provider and Service, 2024. Final-action inpatient discharges for Original (fee-for-service) Medicare. CMS data page
Procedures
DRGs 216–221 cover open valve surgery and some other major cardiothoracic procedures. DRG 218 had no 2024 records. TAVR (DRGs 266–267) is analyzed separately.
What the numbers mean
Charges are hospital list prices, not what patients or insurers typically pay. Each ratio is a hospital's average charge divided by its average Medicare payment for one DRG. Medians are not weighted by number of discharges.
Privacy
CMS leaves out any hospital and DRG pair with 10 or fewer discharges, so a missing hospital may still perform these surgeries.
Cleaning
Started from 145,879 rows. Filtered to DRGs 216–221 and 266–267, split TAVR into its own table, converted dollar fields to numbers, and checked for missing or zero payments (none found). Final tables: 792 open valve rows and 1,223 TAVR rows.
